MT5 accounts

An account is "Connected" only after the hosting machine logs in to it.
Your MT5 password is encrypted on the server (AES-256) before it is saved and is never shown again. It is only sent to your hosting machine to log in. A master password is needed for an EA to trade; an investor password cannot let an EA trade. Prefer demo accounts while testing.
